FAQ & policies

Simple, clear and transparent answers for students, learners and partners.

What is the registration fee?

A one-time registration fee of KES 1,000 applies when enrolling in a course or bundle, unless GTI states otherwise in the checkout instructions.

Do you offer certificates?

Yes. GTI issues certificates upon completion of the course requirements and successful assessment where applicable.

Is this accredited?

Only GTI can confirm any accreditation or formal recognition status. Students should verify the accreditation details before relying on the certificate for regulated roles.

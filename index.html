Common questions

About the college

How do I register for a course?

Tap Register at the top of the page, fill in your name, phone, email and course, and submit. You will see how much to pay. Once we confirm your payment, we send your class access by WhatsApp or email.

Do I need to be online at a set time?

Mostly no. You can study the lessons on your own schedule, from your phone or computer. If a course includes a live session, we send the date and time after you register, and a recording is available if you miss it.

Can I take more than one course?

Yes. You can register for as many courses as you like. The one-time KES 1,000 registration fee applies to each course.

Fees and payment

How much do the courses cost?

Course fees range from KES 2,500 to KES 8,000, and each fee is shown on its course card. The Certificate in Basic Caregiving package is KES 7,500.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. There is a one-time registration fee of KES 1,000 for each course. It gives you access to the course. The course fee is paid in addition, and you can pay both together or separately.

How do I pay?

Pay by M-Pesa Paybill: business number 600100, account number 0100016742947. Or pay by bank transfer to Stanbic Bank, account name Gruis Training Institute, account number 0100016742947, Thika branch. Then send us your confirmation message with your full name and course.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. M-Pesa or your bank sends you a confirmation message as soon as you pay. After we confirm your payment, we send you a Gruis receipt for both the registration fee and the course fee, by WhatsApp or email.

Certificates

How do I receive my certificate?

Once you finish the coursework and pass the final assessment, we email your Gruis Training Institute Certificate of Completion to you as a PDF. It carries your name, course, date of completion, a unique certificate number and a QR code.

Is the certificate government-accredited?

No. It is a Gruis Training Institute Certificate of Completion. It shows what you completed with us, and it is not a government-accredited qualification.

Can an employer check my certificate?

Yes. Every certificate has a unique number and a QR code that opens a verification page. An employer can email gruis.edu@gmail.com or call or WhatsApp +1 (213) 337-8634 with that number and we will confirm it.

Contact

How can I contact you?

WhatsApp or call +1 (213) 337-8634, or email gruis.edu@gmail.com. We are based in Lusail, Doha, Qatar.

Ready to start?

Pay by M-Pesa or bank transfer, then send us your confirmation and the course you chose. We will reply with your class dates.

Job placement support: GCC, Europe, UK and Asia

For job seekers, including GTI graduates, who want to work abroad. We connect you directly with employers, with no agent commissions in between.

  • Guided by an experienced HR team. Our human resources team has extensive experience in GCC employment and established links with employers in the region.
  • Direct employer contacts, no agent commissions. We share job postings together with the company's official contact details.
  • You apply yourself. You contact the employer directly and make your own application.
  • Help with your documents. We prepare your CV and guide you through your passport and certificate of good conduct applications.

Please note: Gruis Training Institute is not a recruitment agent. We do not place workers or take fees from employers. Our HR team uses its experience and employer links to connect you directly with companies, and you apply yourself. We cannot guarantee a job, because employers make their own hiring decisions. Never pay anyone for a job offer, and always confirm an employer's details before you share money or documents.

Africans in Qatar: help with employer and legal-status problems

Stuck in Qatar with a difficult situation? Talk to us first. We review your case and connect you with our trusted partners in Qatar.

We can help if you:

  • Have a complicated problem with your employer, for example after landing in Qatar
  • Have overstayed or have expired residence or visa papers
  • Need to regularise your legal documents
  • Have issues with the Ministry of Interior (MOI)
  • Have issues with the Ministry of Labour (MOL)
  • Want to find a job in Qatar once your papers are in order

Please note: we are not a law firm or a government office, and we cannot promise an outcome. Decisions on documents and status are made by the Qatari authorities. For document regularisation we connect you with our trusted partners in Qatar. Once your papers are in order, our HR team can share direct employer contacts. In an emergency, contact your country's embassy in Doha as well.

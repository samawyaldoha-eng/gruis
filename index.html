Common questions

About the college

How do I register for a course?

Tap Register at the top of the page, fill in your name, phone, email and course, and submit. You will see how much to pay. Once we confirm your payment, we send your class access.

Do I need to be online at a set time?

Mostly no. You can study the lessons on your own schedule, from your phone or computer. If a course includes a live session, we tell you in advance.

Can I take more than one course?

Yes. You can register for as many courses as you like. The one-time KES 1,000 registration fee applies to each course.

Fees and payment

How much do the courses cost?

Course fees range from KES 2,500 to KES 8,000, and each fee is shown on its course card. The Certificate in Basic Caregiving package is KES 7,500.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes. There is a one-time registration fee of KES 1,000 for each course. It gives you access to the course. The course fee is paid in addition.

How do I pay?

Pay by M-Pesa Paybill: business number 600100, account number 0100016742947. Or pay by bank transfer to Stanbic Bank, account name Gruis Training Institute.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. M-Pesa or your bank sends you a confirmation message. After we confirm your payment, we send you a Gruis receipt for your records.

Certificates

How do I receive my certificate?

Once you finish the coursework and pass the final assessment, we email your Gruis Training Institute Certificate of Completion to you.

Is the certificate government-accredited?

No. It is a Gruis Training Institute Certificate of Completion. It shows what you completed with us, and it is not a government-accredited qualification.

Can an employer check my certificate?

Yes. Every certificate has a unique number and QR code that opens a verification page. An employer can ask us to verify it.

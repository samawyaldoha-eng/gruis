Certification & employer trust

We are open and transparent about accreditation status, employer verification, and the value of GTI-issued certificates.

Is GTI accredited?

GTI is currently pursuing NITA accreditation. While that process is underway, GTI issues Certificates of Completion and professional certificates for learners and international employers.

How do employers verify certificates?

Each GTI certificate includes a unique certificate ID and public verification page. Employers can verify completion details before hiring or onboarding.

Can international learners use these certificates?

Yes. GTI certificates are designed for practical skill validation and can be used for employer and recruitment processes while international accreditation continues.

How do we prevent fake or misleading certificates?

GTI uses unique certificate IDs, verification pages, skill-aligned course outcomes, and employer consent-based candidate sharing to improve trust and reduce fraud.
